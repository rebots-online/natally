# screen-atlas-synastry
**Intent:** two people. Bi-wheel (person A inner, person B outer), cross-aspect table (A body · aspect · B body · orb · applying), house overlays (B's planets in A's houses, and the reverse). People picker (two chips). No score, no label.
**Variants:** mobile · desktop · one person without birth time (overlays into that person's houses shown as honest absence; the other direction still computed).
**Content classes:** computed-fact only.
**Journeys:** J4.
