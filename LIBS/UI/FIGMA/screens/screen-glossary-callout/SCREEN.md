# screen-glossary-callout
**Intent:** tapping any glyph or term opens a callout: the glyph, the name, "What it is" and "In synastry" (authored-static education, identical for every user, labelled), and "Ask natally about this" which posts the term into the conversation.
**Variants:** none (content varies by term).
**Content classes:** authored-static, labelled.
**Journeys:** J2, J4.
